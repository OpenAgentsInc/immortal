Postgres, release-load, and fresh-Debian stages.

## Limits

This is a source, dependency, schema, fixture, and local process review. It is
not an external penetration test, production traffic exercise, secret-history
audit, or live-network deployment attestation. The static secret patterns are
high-signal tripwires, not a substitute for hosting-provider secret scanning
or key rotation. Public replacement claims remain gated on their issue-specific
live deployment evidence.
